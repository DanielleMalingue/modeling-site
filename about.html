Bio

The short version.

I'm Cameroonian, raised between Cameroon and the U.S., based in Los Angeles, finishing my last year at USC. I didn't plan on modeling — Nike emailed me on Instagram, and a year later a stranger stopped me at a CVS to say the same thing. Twice was enough to take it seriously.

I'm easy on set. I take direction fast, I'm on time, and I'd rather get the shot than be interesting about it. Commercial, editorial, lifestyle — I'm built for range, and I bring the same attention whether I'm shooting a campaign or a single product card.

When I'm not on camera I'm building Addison — a productivity app I designed and shipped, currently live on the App Store. The two practices feed each other: both ask you to hold a clear vision and obsess over the details no one else notices.
